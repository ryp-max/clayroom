Membership · San Francisco Studios

Open studio time, on your own schedule.

Membership is for people who've already taken an intro class and want standing access to wheels, glazes, and kilns. Studios keep Monday–Saturday 10am–9pm and Sunday 10am–7:30pm hours.

General Membership · Ceramic

Unlimited access to both SF studios.

Includes access to both SoMa and Potrero Hill: 80+ wheels, 60+ glazes across the two studios, and 4 slab rollers. Members get dedicated shelf space and first access to "Power Hour" classes, held 6–8 times a month. Woodshop access is available once certified. 24/7 access can be added for $25/month.
