About

Started with one room in Potrero Hill.

Clayroom opened as a 7,000 sq ft neighborhood studio in Potrero Hill in 2018. The idea was simple: give people a warm, unpretentious place to work with their hands. Four more studios followed: SoMa, San Mateo, and two in Oakland.

Approach

Simplicity, relaxed pacing, room to experiment.

Instruction across all five studios follows the same idea: clay rewards concentration and mindfulness more than it rewards perfection. Classes stay small, capped at 10 students, so instructors can actually watch what your hands are doing instead of lecturing at the whole room.

Community

Studios built around real neighborhoods.

Each location has its own equipment and character: SoMa pairs ceramics with a working woodshop, San Mateo runs on natural light from 17-foot windows, and West Oakland fires both cone 5 and cone 10 under one roof. See the full breakdown on Studios.

W.A.R.P. Artist Residency

A six-month residency for Black emerging ceramic artists.

Clayroom built W.A.R.P. to address a specific gap: the disparity of underrepresented and under-resourced Black people in the ceramic art community. The program is free to accepted residents.

Studio access

A six-month membership, valued at roughly $1,055, across the SF and Oakland facilities.

Materials

$250 worth of clay and materials, plus access to kilns, wheels, 35+ glazes, and a spray booth.

Mentorship

Direct mentorship and instructor training, plus a closing exhibition with an artist talk.

Commitment

Residents give at least 2 hours a week assisting with workshops and studio operations.

How to apply

Submit a 500-word letter of interest, two professional references with contact information, and a portfolio of 5–10 recent works, combined into a single PDF. Applications are due February 20, 2026. Questions go to contact@clayroomsf.com.

Class Scholarships

SoMa ceramics scholarship.

A fully-funded award covering instruction, studio access, clay, firing fees, and glazes for one six-week ceramics course, not woodworking. Clayroom encourages BIPOC and/or LGBTQIA+ applicants, especially those aged 18–25. Up to two scholarships are awarded per person, per year.
